# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project is expected to help keep it welcoming,
respectful, and constructive. Treat people with consideration regardless of
their background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

Examples of behavior that supports this standard include:

- Using considerate, inclusive language.
- Giving and receiving technical feedback respectfully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Acknowledging mistakes and helping repair their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language,
deliberate intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing another
person's private information, and sustained disruption of project
discussions.

## Scope

This standard applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces when someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this standard privately to the project
maintainers through GitHub's private contact or reporting options. Do not
include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will
review reports fairly, keep them confidential where possible, and may edit or
remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently and to explain
enforcement decisions when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety.
